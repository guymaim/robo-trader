# Getting started

## 1. Request access

1. Visit [https://robo-trader.egedsoft.co.il](https://robo-trader.egedsoft.co.il).
2. Sign in with **Google**.
3. You will land on a **pending** screen until an administrator enables your account.

Access is invite-only on purpose: live trading needs review before paper becomes live.

## 2. After you are approved

Once an admin enables your account:

1. Sign in again if needed.
2. Connect your **Alpaca** or **Interactive Brokers** account from the app settings / help pages.
3. Prefer **paper** mode first so you can learn the desk without risking capital.
4. Read the on-site **Risk Disclosure**, Terms, and Privacy pages before trading.

## 3. What to expect

- Orders (when trading is enabled) go to **your** broker account — Robo Trader does not custody funds.
- Position sizing on live paths is **cash-only** (no margin buying power for buys).
- Safeguards such as a kill switch and daily-loss halt are part of the product design.

Exact screens and broker setup steps live in the app’s Help section after you sign in.
