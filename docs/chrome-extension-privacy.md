# Robo Trader Chrome extension: privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-24_

This policy covers the **Robo Trader** extension for Google Chrome. The
Robo Trader service itself is covered by its own privacy policy at
https://robo-trader.egedsoft.co.il/legal/privacy.

## What the extension does

The extension shows the status of your Robo Trader account in the Chrome
toolbar: your traders, equity, today's profit or loss, halts, decisions
waiting for you, and your recent simulation runs. It can also show desktop
alerts if you turn them on. It is read-only and cannot place, change or cancel
trades.

## What data it uses

- **Your Robo Trader account data.** The extension reads your own account
  status from `https://robo-trader.egedsoft.co.il` and shows it in the popup.
  It sends read-only requests to that site and no other.
- **Your Robo Trader sign-in.** Chrome attaches the session cookie you
  already have on the Robo Trader site to those requests. The extension never
  reads, copies or stores that cookie, and it never asks for a password,
  broker API key or other credential.

## What it stores on your computer

In Chrome's local extension storage, on your device only:

- your options (badge on or off, how often to check, alerts on or off), and
- for each of your traders, whether it is halted and how many decisions are
  waiting, so an alert fires only when something starts.

It stores no amounts, names, passwords, API keys or tokens. Removing the
extension deletes this data.

## What it shares

Nothing. The extension sends no data to the extension developer or to anyone
else, and it contains no analytics, advertising or third-party code. The only
network requests it makes are the read-only requests to the Robo Trader site
described above. Those requests are handled under the Robo Trader service's
privacy policy.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for anything other than
showing your Robo Trader status in the extension. We do not use or share it
to decide creditworthiness or for lending.

## Permissions

- **alarms**: to check your status in the background on the schedule you
  choose.
- **storage**: to keep your options and the alert state described above.
- **notifications** (optional): to show desktop alerts, only if you turn them
  on. Turning alerts off removes this permission.
- **Access to robo-trader.egedsoft.co.il**: to read your status from the
  Robo Trader site. The extension cannot read or change any other website.
